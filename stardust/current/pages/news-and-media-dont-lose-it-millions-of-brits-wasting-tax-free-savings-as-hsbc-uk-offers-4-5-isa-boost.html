Don’t lose it: Millions of Brits wasting tax-free savings as HSBC UK offers 4.5%+ ISA boost

New research reveals widespread ISA gap as savers urged to act before tax year end.

New research from HSBC UK reveals although more than half of Brits (54%) are committed tax-free savers during ISA season, there remains a persistent gap in how UK adults are saving for their future.

Close to two in five (39%) admit to not having an ISA as part of their saving or investment portfolio, missing out on valuable tax-free allowances.

The nationally representative survey of over 1,000 UK adults highlights a clear imbalance in ISA adoption across the market:

  • 44% hold a Cash ISA, making it the most widely used product
  • Only 21% have a Stocks & Shares ISA, despite the potential for higher long-term returns
  • 39% do not hold any ISA at all

Among those who do hold ISAs, contribution habits also reveal a strong bias towards cash saving over investing:

  • Nearly half (49%) have contributed only to a Cash ISA in the current tax year
  • Just 18% have contributed only to a Stocks & Shares ISA
  • Only 12% are contributing to both, highlighting a limited approach to diversified saving

Despite the £20,000 annual allowance, relatively few savers are making the most of it, with just 23% saying they expect to use their full ISA allowance this tax year, rising among those aged 55+. On average, ISA holders estimate they expect to save around £11,330, just over half of their allowance.

The UK ISA investment gap

The data shows that while ISAs remain a cornerstone of UK savings behaviour, they are primarily being used for cash-based saving rather than long-term wealth building.

Cash ISAs continue to appeal due to their accessibility, simplicity and perceived safety, whilst the lower uptake of Stocks & Shares ISAs points to a consistent investment gap amongst UK adults, with many not making the most of the benefits of a diversified portfolio of savings and investments to build long-term wealth. In fact, 22% of respondents said they did not know that they could hold a Cash ISA and stocks and shares ISA in the same tax year, pointing to education being a barrier to people using their allowance.

HSBC UK strengthens ISA offering

HSBC UK has strengthened its ISA offering and launched a market-leading rate and cash incentive to help more customers take advantage of tax-efficient savings during ISA season late last year, it also refreshed its digital wealth offering to make opening a stocks and shares ISA through the app a quick and seamless experience.

The Bank’s Fixed Rate Cash ISA now offers a leading rate of 4.5%, combined with a recently launched ISA incentive for eligible customers, designed to encourage both new and existing savers to make the most of their ISA allowance before the deadline.

This means customers can not only benefit from tax-free interest, but also receive an additional boost through cashback, at a time when maximising returns is front of mind for many households.

The tiered incentive is based on the total amount of new funds added during the offer period which runs cross tax year:

  • Those depositing £20,000 - £49,999 will get £150 into their HSBC current account
  • Those depositing £50,000 - £99,999 will get £250 into their HSBC current account
  • Those depositing £100,000+ will get £500 into their HSBC current account

Lloyd Robson, Head of Savings at HSBC UK, said:

“Our research shows that while nearly half of UK adults are using Cash ISAs, a significant proportion are still missing out entirely on the benefits of tax-free saving.

“ISAs are one of the most effective tools available to help people build financial resilience, yet the vast majority are either not using them or if they are, not utilising their full allowance. That represents a major opportunity for people to take greater control of their financial future.

“There is also a clear gap between saving and investing. While cash plays an important role, it’s important that people feel supported and confident to consider a broader range of options that could help their money grow over the longer term.”

HSBC UK is supporting customers with a range of ISA options tailored to different financial goals, from easy-access Cash ISAs for short-term needs to Stocks and Shares ISA for those looking to grow their wealth over time.
